Community · mentoring · conservation

Volunteering & outreach

Outside of coursework and research, I care a lot about making science feel less distant and more accessible—especially for students who don’t always see themselves represented in STEM, and for coastal ecosystems that are usually out of sight and out of mind.

A lot of this has looked like slow, unglamorous work: writing letters, planting saplings, cleaning up shorelines, and helping younger students connect school science to real ecosystems.

STEM mentoring Marine & coastal Science communication

What this work has taught me

  • How to explain technical ideas without losing nuance.

  • That small, local projects still matter for climate and oceans.

  • How much younger students light up when science feels like theirs.

Letters to a Pre-Scientist

STEM pen-pal mentoring program

Remote · 2024–present

I write letters to a middle-school student about science, university life, and what it’s like to study oceans and data. We talk about everything from parrotfish and whale sharks to what research actually looks like day-to-day.

  • Demystify STEM paths and share honest snapshots of university life.
  • Explain marine biology and data science concepts in accessible ways.
  • Encourage curiosity, questions, and a sense that science is “for them.”

Marine conservation events

Blue Crew & local partners

UAE · 2022–2024

Through the marine conservation club I founded in high school, I helped organize mangrove planting and monitoring days, as well as shoreline clean-ups with local environmental groups.

  • Coordinated student volunteers for mangrove planting and monitoring.
  • Worked with local partners on logistics, safety, and site selection.
  • Explained why habitats like mangroves matter for climate resilience.

Science outreach & peer support

Campus & community spaces

Ongoing

I look for small ways to make science feel more collaborative—helping friends debug code, sharing research experiences with younger students, or talking through project ideas with people who are just starting to explore STEM.

  • Support peers with statistics, coding, and data-cleaning questions.
  • Share application and research tips with students a step behind me.
  • Help connect environmental science topics to real-world examples.
