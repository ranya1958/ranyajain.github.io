Volunteering & Community Work

Conservation · Mentoring · Science outreach

Outside of coursework and research, I care a lot about how science and environmental issues show up in everyday life. Most of my volunteering revolves around conservation work, mentoring students, and helping make science feel more accessible and less intimidating.

In high school, I founded a marine conservation club and helped organize mangrove planting and monitoring events in the UAE, partnering with local environmental groups. More recently, I’ve volunteered as a pen pal with Letters to a Pre-Scientist and with student-facing programs that support first-generation and underrepresented students navigating STEM pathways.

These experiences have shaped how I think about “impact”: not just doing analysis or producing results, but building spaces where people feel invited into science and where environmental work is grounded in real communities.

Selected roles

  • Founder, high school marine conservation club – organized mangrove plantation and monitoring events with local NGOs.
  • Volunteer, coastal restoration and marine education events focused on habitat health and stewardship.
  • Letters to a Pre-Scientist – long-term pen pal, writing about marine biology, data, and what science looks like day to day.
  • Student mentoring and outreach – helping younger students think through classes, applications, and finding their place in STEM.
