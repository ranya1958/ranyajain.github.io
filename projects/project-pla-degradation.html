Effect of Temperature on Chemical Recycling of Polylactic Acid

PLA hydrolysis · Colorimetry · Beer–Lambert law

In this exploration, I investigated how temperature affects the chemical recycling of polylactic acid (PLA) in a 50% ethanol–NaOH solution by measuring lactic acid production at different temperatures. After hydrolysis, lactic acid was complexed with iron(III) chloride and quantified using a colorimeter and the Beer–Lambert law.

Across the tested range, lactic acid concentration increased with temperature. Overall, this project strengthened my understanding of polymer degradation, experimental design, and how to connect lab-scale reaction conditions to questions about greener plastics and end-of-life options for biodegradable materials.

Project details

  • Focus: hydrolysis of PLA in 50% ethanol–NaOH
  • Methods: colorimetry, calibration curve, Beer–Lambert law, uncertainty propagation
  • Themes: green chemistry, biodegradable polymers, experimental limitations in real lab settings
